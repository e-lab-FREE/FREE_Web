The Elab is a space where real cientific experiments can be executed by anyone, anytime, anywehere. Most of the available experiments were designed, buit and assembled by at Instituto Superior Tecnico and are deployed in the whole world.

Some of these experiments are being user in real teaching/leaning context by schools in europe and in the Americas. Nonethless, any user on the Internet can enter elab, select one of the available experiments, configure it and see in real time its operations. Resulting data are yours to keep them!
